HOW TO OPEN NOTCHI
==================

1. Drag NotChi into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open NotChi from Applications.
   macOS will warn that it can't check the app for malware.
   This happens because NotChi is a free, source-available project
   without a paid Apple Developer ID. Click "Done".

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to "Security" and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the NotChi message. Enter your password, then click "Open".

   (The button only shows for about an hour after step 2.
   If it's missing, open NotChi again and come back.)

You only need to do this once.


USING NOTCHI
------------

• Move your pointer onto the notch (or press ⌥⌘N) to open it.
  NotChi has no Dock icon; it lives in the notch.

• For one-click paste from your clipboard history, allow
  System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → NotChi.

• Settings: click the gear icon in the open notch.
